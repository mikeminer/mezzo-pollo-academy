Monthly subscription

The website offers a monthly pass for exactly 0.2 SOL, lasting 30 days from preparation of the purchase transaction. There is no automatic renewal. It provides ten starting lives per subscription round, a slower pace and optional participation in the social score board. Each round starts with ten lives; an ongoing round retains its remaining lives if access expires.

With your explicit wallet approval, one atomic Solana transaction buys MEMEZZO and creates a 30-day Token-2022 timelock in the existing DevFridge program. The mint is Dv1prgxPZs1M6vpacCzmGjLkd7ZFZVJSHCH9PLwEpump. The program is 9RY54dNPYTzDyh3TfFqDdt2b2KMM56KW1tw9erRTGQo6. Default slippage is 2%. The guaranteed minimum swap output is deposited; any extra tokens remain in your wallet. The 0.2 SOL is used for the token purchase, not transferred as a subscription fee to the publisher. Network fees and account creation rent are additional and shown before approval. A small SOL reserve is required but not charged.

The transaction either completes both purchase and timelock or neither; a failed transaction may still incur a network fee. Token quantities depend on market conditions and their value can fall. Tokens cannot be withdrawn before the recorded expiry. Expiry does not automatically return tokens or convert them into SOL. Redemption is a separate external DevFridge operation, subject to its current conditions, including a 2% PASTA buy-and-burn fee, network fees and an executable market route. A route may be unavailable even after expiry. The game does not perform redemption.

Access requires at least one verified active deposit of MEMEZZO belonging to the connected wallet, regardless of amount. Existing external deposits also qualify. The latest valid expiry controls access; multiple purchases do not add their durations together. Verification runs when connecting, returning to the foreground and at expiry. Unavailable verification cannot grant access. The currently supported purchase route is the live Pump bonding curve; after graduation, purchases remain unavailable until a reviewed new route is supported.

Payment recovery information is kept on this browser. Do not clear site data while a payment is pending. If the result is uncertain, use Check payment rather than purchasing again. A confirmed matching deposit can recover the purchase without a returned signature. An unresolved result remains pending; contact support if it cannot be reconciled.

Community and sharing

The subscription leaderboard contains player-reported scores. A wallet signature verifies authorship, not honest gameplay. It is a social feature, not an authoritative competition, and awards no prizes. Publication is optional and makes the wallet address, highest shared score and update time public. Only currently recorded active subscriptions are listed. Profile links open Cherry; following or contacting another player is your decision and may require a Cherry account.

Arcade is purchased separately per round. Players may share a score photo on X with #mezzopollo or on the MEMEZZO Pump.fun page for a chance to be contacted about a discretionary SOL prize. Posting, paying or achieving a score does not guarantee selection or payment. The game does not automatically publish, contact users or distribute prizes.
