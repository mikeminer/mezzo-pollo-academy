Website subscription privacy

Website update: 17 November 2026. Publisher: Pastaman (Michele Angelo Forlani). Contact: welcome@devfridge.cool.

Wallet and payments

The game requests your public wallet address, never a seed phrase or private key. The website sends that address to its subscription and purchase endpoints on Vercel. Server-side Helius RPC checks public Solana accounts and simulates the unsigned purchase. The Helius credential stays on the server. Vercel and Helius process normal network/operational information under their own policies. Actual transactions are approved and submitted through your chosen wallet and become public blockchain records.

Language, tutorial progress, settings and local best scores stay in browser storage. A pending monthly purchase stores its wallet, mint, plan, amount, expiry, deposit address and any returned signature locally for recovery. It does not store private keys. Clearing browser data removes this recovery record. Account changes or disconnecting remove in-memory access, but do not erase a pending payment.

Optional public community

Only if you choose Prepare publication and Sign and publish does the website request a signature of a score-publication message. This is not a transaction. The server verifies the signature and subscription, then stores the public wallet address, highest shared score, subscription expiry and update time in the project's hosted Postgres database. Unconsumed challenges expire after five minutes. Expired challenges are cleaned on subsequent publication requests. The public list includes up to 100 entries whose recorded subscription expiry is still in the future. Stored scores may remain after they disappear from that list; contact welcome@devfridge.cool to request removal. On-chain records cannot be removed by the publisher.

Rate limiting uses a hashed connection identifier. No signature or wallet address is used as proof of honest gameplay. A Cherry profile link is constructed from the public wallet address; the game does not automatically follow users, send messages or query private Cherry profiles. Opening Cherry, X, Pump.fun or the Solana dApp Store sends you to a separate service with its own privacy practices. You choose whether to upload any score image.
