Android Privacy Policy

MezzoPollo — Android 1.0.2 Last updated: 27 September 2026

The free demo starts each new game with 2 lives and needs no wallet or network connection. Game code, fonts, soundtrack and English subtitles are included in the app.

Your language, music/caption choices and unverified records stay in app storage on this device. Demo and MEMEZZO records are separate. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes them. Android backup is disabled. There is no analytics, advertising, login server, leaderboard or prize system.

Only when you choose to connect a wallet does Android open an installed Mobile Wallet Adapter compatible wallet. This app requests your public Solana account, never a seed phrase, private key, message signature or transaction. Connection authorizations are held in memory and are not stored by the app. Wallet apps manage their own authorization records.

Checking main-game access sends the public wallet address and MEMEZZO mint to https://scan.devfridge.cool/api/sdk/check over HTTPS. That service receives network information such as your IP address and operates under its own policies. The main game requires at least 1,000,000 active timelocked MEMEZZO; each full million gives one total starting life. No early withdrawals; DevFridge redemption has a 2% PASTA buy-and-burn fee plus network costs and needs an executable Jupiter route. Wallet features are intended for adults.

External links open your browser and may contact third-party services under their own policies. Opening external links is optional. The app does not create or redeem timelocks.
