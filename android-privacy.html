Android Privacy Policy

MezzoPollo — Android 1.3.2 Last updated: 21 October 2026

MezzoPollo has two game modes: Arcade and Subscription. Game code, all translations, fonts, soundtrack and English subtitles are included in the app. Its 25 language/country profiles and interactive tutorial work locally. The installed game does not load game content from Vercel. In Android 1.3.2, payment verification uses the protected MezzoPollo RPC relay hosted on Vercel. Full runs require an Arcade payment or an eligible Subscription timelock.

Your language, tutorial completion, music/caption choices and unverified records stay in app storage on this device. Records distinguish mode and Arcade difficulty. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes them. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. There is no analytics, login server, leaderboard or prize system. Free mode has 1 life and requires no wallet or payment. A bundled OnlyFridge Club promotion is shown between Free rounds, without an advertising SDK, tracking pixel or ad-network request. You may continue without opening it. Only an explicit tap opens the Solana dApp Store listing or official download page, which operates under its own privacy policy. Arcade and Subscription do not show this promotion.

Choosing Connect in Subscription or purchasing an Arcade run opens an installed Mobile Wallet Adapter compatible wallet. The app requests your public Solana account, never a seed phrase or private key. Subscription verification does not request a transaction. Only an explicit Arcade purchase asks your wallet to approve and submit a SOL transfer. Connection authorizations and unlocked Subscription lives are held in memory, not stored by the app. Wallet apps manage their own authorization records.

Arcade charges for each run: Medium costs 0.002 SOL for 3 lives; Easy 0.003 SOL for 9 lives; God 0.01 SOL for 32 lives. Network fees are additional. The fixed recipient is AXomB2bpJ7F3EFVPQwrwBV4yVE7bazshZaVa64KhGCfE. The wallet signs a transfer with a unique run reference and memo. The public Solana blockchain records the sender, recipient, amount, reference, memo and transaction signature. No reward or payout is offered.

Native Arcade 1.3.3 communicates over HTTPS with https://mezzopollo.it/api/native-arcade-rpc to prepare and verify finalized payments through Alchemy Solana RPC. The relay and its hosting provider Vercel receive public addresses, transaction references/signatures and network information such as your IP address. Alchemy receives the permitted blockchain queries from the relay. The private Alchemy API credential stays on the server. Neither the relay nor Alchemy receives your wallet private key or local recovery secret. Earlier Android 1.3.1 installations use the public Solana RPC endpoint until updated. Internet access and a compatible wallet are needed for purchases and confirmation. A local private database stores payment intents, recovery secrets, prepared transaction information and verified receipts. One confirmed receipt starts at most one run in the installed app. Unused paid credit can be recovered after restarting the app, but clearing app data or uninstalling removes this local recovery record. The game continues locally after admission; there is no continuous payment verification while playing.

Only an explicit Connect or Check locks action inside Subscription sends the public wallet address and MEMEZZO mint to https://scan.devfridge.cool/api/sdk/check over HTTPS. That service receives network information such as your IP address and operates under its own policies. Playing, restarting, pausing, resuming or returning from the background never requests another token check.

A successful check unlocks slower Subscription runs for this app session when at least 10,000,000 MEMEZZO are in qualifying active DevFridge timelocks. Each full million gives one total starting life, so the minimum eligible amount gives 10 lives. There are no additional base lives. Existing runs keep their assigned lives. You may deliberately check again; wallet changes and disconnection clear the entitlement. Closing or reloading the game also clears the in-memory entitlement. Subscription is a timelock eligibility rule, not an automatic recurring charge. The token mint is Dv1prgxPZs1M6vpacCzmGjLkd7ZFZVJSHCH9PLwEpump.

When you choose to share a result, the app creates a PNG with the score and game difficulty and opens the Android share chooser with that image and accompanying text. You choose the destination and confirm sending. The app keeps a temporary image in its private cache and grants the chosen sharing app access to that image. Sharing services have their own policies.

Tokens are timelocked externally on DevFridge, not spent or transferred to this app. External services and wallet applications operate under their own terms. Wallet features are intended for adults. External links open your browser under third-party policies. The app does not create or redeem timelocks.
