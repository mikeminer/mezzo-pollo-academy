Android Privacy Policy

MezzoPollo — Android 1.3.0 Last updated: 19 October 2026

Hard mode starts each new game with 2 lives and needs no wallet or token verification. A guided tutorial teaches the real controls and rules across 25 language/country profiles. Game code, all translations, fonts, soundtrack and English subtitles are included in the app for offline play. The installed game does not load its content or Arcade payment service from Vercel.

Your language, tutorial completion, music/caption choices and unverified records stay in app storage on this device. Records distinguish game mode and Arcade difficulty. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes them. Android cloud backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. There is no analytics, login server, leaderboard or prize system.

Coinzilla delivery remains disabled in this build: no advertising requests or impressions are sent. The optional sponsored-card integration is limited to regular Hard-mode runs; tutorials, Easy mode and resume do not show it. Activation still requires publisher/placement approval and updated privacy disclosures. Hard mode always starts with 2 lives.

Choosing Connect in the optional shop or purchasing an Arcade run opens an installed Mobile Wallet Adapter compatible wallet. The app requests your public Solana account, never a seed phrase or private key. Shop verification does not request a transaction. Only an explicit Arcade purchase asks your wallet to approve and submit a SOL transfer. Connection authorizations and unlocked Easy lives are held in memory, not stored by the app. Wallet apps manage their own authorization records.

Arcade is optional and charges for each run: Hard costs 0.001 SOL for 1 life; Medium 0.002 SOL for 3 lives; Easy 0.003 SOL for 9 lives; God 0.01 SOL for 32 lives. Network fees are additional. The fixed recipient is AXomB2bpJ7F3EFVPQwrwBV4yVE7bazshZaVa64KhGCfE. The wallet signs a transfer with a unique run reference and memo. The public Solana blockchain records the sender, recipient, amount, reference, memo and transaction signature. No reward or payout is offered.

Native Arcade communicates directly over HTTPS with https://api.mainnet-beta.solana.com to prepare and verify finalized payments. This RPC service receives the public addresses, transaction references/signatures and network information such as your IP address. It does not receive a private key or the local recovery secret. Internet access and a compatible wallet are needed for purchases and confirmation. A local private database stores payment intents, recovery secrets, prepared transaction information and verified receipts. One confirmed receipt starts at most one run in the installed app. Unused paid credit can be recovered after restarting the app, but clearing app data or uninstalling removes this local recovery record. The game continues locally after admission; there is no continuous payment verification while playing.

When you choose to share a result, the app creates a PNG with the score and game difficulty and opens the Android share chooser with that image and accompanying text. You choose the destination and confirm sending. The app keeps a temporary image in its private cache and grants the chosen sharing app access to that image. Sharing services have their own policies.

Only an explicit Connect or Check locks action inside the shop sends the public wallet address and MEMEZZO mint to https://scan.devfridge.cool/api/sdk/check over HTTPS. That service receives network information such as your IP address and operates under its own policies. Playing, restarting, pausing, resuming or returning from the background never requests another token check.

A successful shop check unlocks slower Easy mode for this app session, with one total starting life per full million MEMEZZO in qualifying active timelocks at check time (minimum 1,000,000). There are no additional base lives in Easy. Existing runs keep their assigned lives. You may deliberately check again in the shop; wallet changes and disconnection clear the shop entitlement. Closing or reloading the game also clears the in-memory entitlement.

Tokens are timelocked externally on DevFridge, not spent or transferred to this app. No early withdrawals; DevFridge redemption has a 2% PASTA buy-and-burn fee plus network costs and needs an executable Jupiter route. Wallet features are intended for adults. External links open your browser under third-party policies. The app does not create or redeem timelocks.
