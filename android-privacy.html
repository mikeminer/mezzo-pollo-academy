Android Privacy Policy

MezzoPollo — Android 1.1.0 Last updated: 5 October 2026

Hard mode starts each new game with 2 lives and needs no wallet or token verification. A guided tutorial teaches the real controls and rules in Italian, English or Polish. Game code, fonts, soundtrack and English subtitles are included in the app for offline play.

Your language, tutorial completion, music/caption choices and unverified records stay in app storage on this device. Hard and Easy records are separate. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes them. Android backup is disabled. There is no analytics, login server, leaderboard or prize system.

Coinzilla delivery remains disabled in this build: no advertising requests or impressions are sent. The optional sponsored-card integration is limited to regular Hard-mode runs; tutorials, Easy mode and resume do not show it. Activation still requires publisher/placement approval and updated privacy disclosures. Hard mode always starts with 2 lives.

Only when you open the optional shop and choose Connect does Android open an installed Mobile Wallet Adapter compatible wallet. The app requests your public Solana account, never a seed phrase, private key, message signature or transaction. Connection authorizations and unlocked Easy lives are held in memory, not stored by the app. Wallet apps manage their own authorization records.

Only an explicit Connect or Check locks action inside the shop sends the public wallet address and MEMEZZO mint to https://scan.devfridge.cool/api/sdk/check over HTTPS. That service receives network information such as your IP address and operates under its own policies. Playing, restarting, pausing, resuming or returning from the background never requests another token check.

A successful shop check unlocks slower Easy mode for this app session, with one total starting life per full million MEMEZZO in qualifying active timelocks at check time (minimum 1,000,000). There are no additional base lives in Easy. Existing runs keep their assigned lives. You may deliberately check again in the shop; wallet changes and disconnection clear the shop entitlement. Closing or reloading the game also clears the in-memory entitlement.

Tokens are timelocked externally on DevFridge, not spent or transferred to this app. No early withdrawals; DevFridge redemption has a 2% PASTA buy-and-burn fee plus network costs and needs an executable Jupiter route. Wallet features are intended for adults. External links open your browser under third-party policies. The app does not create or redeem timelocks.
