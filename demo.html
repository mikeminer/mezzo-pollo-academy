Access and limitations

This build is free practice only. No token has launched, no wallet is connected and no DevFridge gate or transaction is implemented. Local scores are unverified; there are no leaderboards, prizes or on-chain rewards. Planned token features are for adults and would unlock cosmetics and seasonal challenges only.

Any future lock would have no early withdrawal and a 2% redemption fee for PASTA buy-and-burn, plus network fees. Redemption of non-PASTA tokens needs an executable Jupiter route. The future mint, minimum amount, aggregation rules and token compatibility remain undecided or unverified.
